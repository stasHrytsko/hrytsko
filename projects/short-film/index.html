The idea

Short films belong in the same collection as software and games. They ask for a different kind of attention: a character, a conflict, a scene that makes you want to see what happens next.

A question worth asking

What makes the scene necessary?

A short film has little room for a scene that only explains. This page will show the creative decisions behind the story as well as my specific contribution to the production.

What belongs here

The project page will hold the actual title, a one-sentence logline, my role, and an excerpt or storyboard. A finished film will appear here when there is one to watch.

The next concrete step

Add the film’s real logline, current stage, credits, and a shareable piece of the work.
