The idea

A board game makes the work tangible: rules, pieces, choices, and people around a table. This is where the development of my game will live alongside the digital projects.

A question worth asking

Is the choice interesting?

A rule can make sense on paper and still fail at the table. The useful evidence is what players actually do, where they get stuck, and what makes them want another round.

What belongs here

The real entry will include the premise, a prototype or rules excerpt, and observations from playtesting. Player counts and playing time will be added when established.
