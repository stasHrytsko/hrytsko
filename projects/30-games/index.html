The idea

The premise is simple: make one small game a day for 30 days. Each day is a constraint on scope, not an excuse to skip the part that makes a game interesting.

A question worth asking

What deserves the next hour?

A game needs a playable core before it needs more features. The experiment puts that choice under pressure: keep the essential interaction, test it, and decide what to leave out.

Daily log

Every day gets its own card and its own page: a date, a title, a small geometric cover in place of a screenshot, and a link through to what I tried and what I'd change. The calendar starts when the experiment actually begins.
