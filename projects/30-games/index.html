What this is

A structured product discovery experiment: 30 mechanics, consistent constraints, public behavioral data. It is a personal side project—a place to explore game design with the same discipline I bring to product and delivery work.

Working principle

Build quickly. Measure honestly. Keep what works.

How the experiment works

01 · Build

Each prototype isolates one core mechanic and is built within the same tight scope.

02 · Play

Players get a working build with minimal explanation and five fixed levels to complete.

03 · Measure

Anonymous events show whether people understand, retry, want more and come back.

04 · Decide

Player votes add preference; comparable behavioral evidence determines what advances.

A fair comparison

Every prototype follows the same rules: one mechanic, deterministic gameplay, five fixed levels, no meta-progression and no monetisation. Ideas change; the testing conditions do not.

How the winner is chosen

The decision is based on four questions: Do players understand the mechanic? Do they retry after failure? Do they want more content? Do they return? Votes and comments explain preference and reveal what to improve, but no single vanity metric decides the result.

After all 30 prototypes have been tested under comparable conditions, the strongest candidates move into a final round. The winning mechanic becomes a real game.

What’s ready

The hub structure, 30-slot schedule and analytics instrumentation are in place. The first playable build and PostHog project token are the next milestones.

Prototype log

Thirty numbered slots, shuffled on every visit to avoid position bias. Each published card includes a cover, its one-sentence rule and a direct Play button.
