The work between the work

Delivery is about the decisions, dependencies, and conversations that connect an idea to a release. My role is to make that work visible and help the people involved move together.

I care about a plan people can use, clear ownership, and knowing when something needs to change.

How I approach delivery

Make the outcome clear

Start with what needs to happen and why. Agree on what a useful result looks like before the work becomes a long list of tasks.

Connect the moving parts

Understand who depends on whom, make ownership explicit, and bring the right people into decisions early enough to matter.

Keep decisions close to reality

Make progress and problems visible. When the situation changes, adjust the plan and explain the trade-offs.

Follow through to the result

A completed task is one part of the picture. The outcome needs to work for the people who depend on it.
