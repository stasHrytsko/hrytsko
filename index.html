About

I moved into software delivery in 2019 after eight years in procurement at Procter & Gamble, Carlsberg, Jacobs Douwe Egberts and Bayer. That background still shapes how I work: contracts, vendors, budgets and launch readiness are part of delivery, not somebody else’s problem.

Today I run regulated-market launches at BrainRocket in Valencia, leading a team of one Program Manager and four Project Managers and reporting to VP and C-level leadership. Before that, I owned the Securrency / DTCC Digital Assets account at Dev.Pro.
